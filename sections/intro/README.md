# Mission Briefing

Before you start the first module, take ten minutes for this briefing. It tells you what this course trains you to do, how to get your machine ready, and who builds the course. You will not touch Istio here, but these three short pages make every page after them easier to follow.

The briefing has three pages, and they are best read in this order:

1. **[Welcome To The Course](./course-01-welcome.md)**: who this course is for, what you can do at the end, how the course is laid out, and how to read a page.
2. **[Get Your Machine Ready](./course-02-before-your-first-launch.md)**: the tools you need, the `astrona` commands you will use every day, and where to get help.
3. **[How This Course Is Made](./course-03-how-this-course-is-made.md)**: how the content is written and checked, who maintains it, and how to report a mistake.

When you are done, start with [section 010, Workload Identity And Mutual TLS](../section-010/README.md).
